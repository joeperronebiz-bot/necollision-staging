Collision repair for Guilford drivers

Getting here from Guilford

  1. From Town Hall on Park Street, take Church Street (Route 77) to I-95 South, about 12 miles on the highway.
  2. Keep left for exit 47 toward Downtown New Haven, onto the connector to Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard.
  3. Turn right onto Ella T. Grasso Boulevard (Route 10), then left onto Derby Avenue (Route 34) for about a mile to 465.
